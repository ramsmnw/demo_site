================================================================================
                         BREAKOUT CONDITIONS
================================================================================

SWING:
• Bullish: close > swingHigh AND high > swingHigh
• Bearish: close < swingLow AND low < swingLow

SUPPORT/RESISTANCE:
• Bullish: close > resistance AND high > resistance
• Bearish: close < support AND low < support

PIVOT:
• Bullish: close > pivot AND high > pivot
• Bearish: close < pivot AND low < pivot

TRIANGLE (if added):
• Ascending: Breakout above flat resistance = BULLISH
• Descending: Breakdown below flat support = BEARISH
• Symmetrical: Direction of breakout determines signal

CHANNEL (if added):
• Ascending: Breakout above = Continuation, Below = Reversal
• Descending: Breakdown below = Continuation, Above = Reversal
• Horizontal: Either direction = New trend
